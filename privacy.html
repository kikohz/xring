XRing 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

XRing 不收集你的任何数据。戒指记录的所有内容都只保存在你的 iPhone 上，我们看不到，也拿不到。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。XRing 没有账号，没有我们自己的服务器，没有广告，没有统计分析，也没有集成任何第三方 SDK。

App 在你的手机上保存什么

为了显示你的健康数据，XRing 会在本机保存：

  • 戒指同步来的数据：步数、距离、消耗、睡眠、心率、HRV、血氧、皮肤温度、压力
  • 你记录的运动，包括户外运动的路线
  • 你填写的个人资料：昵称、身高、体重、年龄、性别，以及你设定的目标
  • App 的设置

这些数据保存在 App 自己的存储空间里，受 iPhone 的系统加密保护。如果你打开了 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份，它们会随整机备份一起保存，这部分由 Apple 按它的隐私政策处理。

App 使用的权限

  • 蓝牙：用来查找、连接戒指并同步数据。App 在后台时也会保持连接，这样数据不会断。
  • 定位：只在你进行户外运动时使用，用来记录路线、距离和配速。位置只保存在本机的那条运动记录里。运动结束后 App 不再获取位置。
  • 苹果「健康」：默认关闭。你在设置里打开后，XRing 会把戒指记录的心率、睡眠、活动和体能训练写入「健康」，并可以读取身高、体重、年龄和性别来填写个人资料。通过「健康」得到的数据不会上传，不会用于广告或营销，也不会提供给任何第三方。你可以随时在系统的「健康」App 里撤销授权。

由 Apple 提供的功能

  • 今日小结：由 iPhone 本机的 Apple 智能生成，数据不离开你的手机。
  • 地图：运动路线使用 Apple 地图显示。加载地图时，Apple 会收到要显示的区域，这部分按 Apple 的隐私政策处理。

导出和诊断

你可以在「设置 › 隐私与数据」里把健康数据导出成一个文件。文件交给谁、存在哪里，完全由你决定。

排查戒指问题时，你可以手动打开蓝牙原始数据记录。它默认关闭，记录只保存在本机，里面包含健康读数。只有你主动导出并发送，别人才能看到。

删除你的数据

从 iPhone 上删除 XRing，App 保存的全部数据会一起删除。已经写入苹果「健康」的记录需要在「健康」App 里另外删除。

儿童

XRing 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和上面的生效日期。如果将来 App 开始收集任何数据，我们会在收集之前先在 App 内说明并征得你的同意。

联系我们

对这份政策有疑问，请在 GitHub 上给我们留言。

XRing Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

XRing does not collect any of your data. Everything your ring records stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it or access it.

What we collect

Nothing. XRing has no accounts, no servers of our own, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores on your iPhone

To show you your health data, XRing stores the following on your device:

  • Data synced from your ring: steps, distance, energy, sleep, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, skin temperature and stress
  • Workouts you record, including the route of outdoor workouts
  • The profile you enter: name, height, weight, age and sex, and the goals you set
  • App settings

This data lives in the app's own storage and is protected by iPhone's system encryption. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, it is included in your device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Permissions the app uses

  • Bluetooth: to find, connect to and sync with your ring. The connection is kept in the background so your data has no gaps.
  • Location: only while an outdoor workout is in progress, to record route, distance and pace. Locations are stored only in that workout on your device. The app stops reading your location when the workout ends.
  • Apple Health: off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, XRing saves heart rate, sleep, activity and workouts recorded by your ring to Apple Health, and can read your height, weight, age and sex to fill in your profile. Data obtained through Apple Health is never uploaded, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with any third party. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Features provided by Apple

  • Today's summary: generated on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. The data does not leave your device.
  • Maps: workout routes are shown with Apple Maps. When a map loads, Apple receives the area being displayed, which is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Export and diagnostics

You can export your health data to a file in Settings › Privacy & data. Who you give that file to and where you keep it is entirely up to you.

When troubleshooting a ring, you can turn on recording of raw Bluetooth data. It is off by default, stays on your device and includes health readings. Nobody else sees it unless you export it and send it yourself.

Deleting your data

Deleting XRing from your iPhone deletes everything the app has stored. Records already saved to Apple Health must be deleted separately in the Health app.

Children

XRing is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting any data, we will explain it in the app and ask for your consent first.
