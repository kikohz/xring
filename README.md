# XRing 技术支持

*R20 / R21 系列智能戒指的配套 App*

遇到问题先看下面的常见问题。没解决的话，在 [GitHub](https://github.com/kikohz/xring/issues) 上给我们留言，我们会尽快回复。

## 找不到戒指，或者连不上
- 确认 iPhone 的蓝牙已打开，并且在「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 蓝牙」里允许了 XRing。
- 给戒指充一会儿电，确认它有电。
- 戒指同一时间只能连一个 App。如果装了别的戒指 App，先把它完全退出。
- 让戒指靠近手机，然后在 App 里重新添加。

## 数据没有更新
- 首页右上角显示「已连接」时才会同步。没连上的话，把 App 切到前台等几秒。
- 心率、血氧这些是戒指按间隔自动测的，不是每一秒都有读数。测量间隔可以在戒指设置里调。
- 睡眠数据要等你起床后一段时间，戒指整理完才会出现。

## 苹果「健康」里没有数据
- 在 XRing 的「设置 › 苹果健康」里打开开关，并在弹出的授权页里允许要写入的类型。
- 之前点过拒绝的话，到系统「健康」App › 右上角头像 › App › XRing 里重新打开。

## 户外运动没有路线

记录路线需要定位权限。到「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 定位服务 › XRing」里选「使用 App 期间」。

## 还没有戒指，想先看看

打开 App，在首页点「查看演示」，可以用示例数据体验全部功能。点顶部的「退出演示」返回。

## 怎么导出或删除我的数据

在「设置 › 隐私与数据」里可以导出健康数据。从 iPhone 上删除 XRing 会删掉 App 保存的全部数据。详细说明见[隐私政策](PRIVACY.md#zh)。

## 联系我们

在 [GitHub](https://github.com/kikohz/xring/issues) 上新建一条留言，写上手机型号、系统版本、戒指型号和遇到的情况。请不要在留言里贴健康数据。

XRing 不是医疗器械，数据仅供日常健康参考。身体不适请咨询医生。

# XRing Support

*Companion app for R20 / R21 series smart rings*

Check the common questions below first. If that doesn't solve it, leave us a message on [GitHub](https://github.com/kikohz/xring/issues) and we'll get back to you.

## The ring isn't found or won't connect
- Make sure Bluetooth is on, and that XRing is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth.
- Charge the ring for a while to make sure it has power.
- The ring can talk to only one app at a time. If you have another ring app installed, quit it completely first.
- Keep the ring close to your iPhone and add it again in the app.

## My data isn't updating
- Syncing happens while the Today tab shows "Connected". If it doesn't, bring the app to the foreground and wait a few seconds.
- Heart rate and blood oxygen are measured automatically at intervals, not every second. You can change the interval in the ring settings.
- Sleep data appears a while after you get up, once the ring has finished processing the night.

## Nothing shows up in Apple Health
- Turn it on in XRing under Settings › Apple Health, and allow the types you want saved on the permission sheet.
- If you declined earlier, open the Health app › your picture › Apps › XRing and turn the types back on.

## My outdoor workout has no route

Routes need location access. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › XRing and choose "While Using the App".

## I don't have a ring yet

Open the app and tap "See a demo" on the Today tab to explore every feature with sample data. Tap "Exit demo" at the top to go back.

## Exporting or deleting my data

You can export your health data in Settings › Privacy & data. Deleting XRing from your iPhone deletes everything the app has stored. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md#en) for details.

## Contact

Open a new issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/kikohz/xring/issues) with your iPhone model, iOS version, ring model and what happened. Please don't post health data in the issue.

XRing is not a medical device. The data is for general wellness only. Talk to a doctor if you feel unwell.
